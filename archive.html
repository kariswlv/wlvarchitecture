A major milestone for WLVArchitecture 🎉🏛️

19/06/2026

Following a full two-day RIBA Visiting Board visit, both of our Architecture courses have received successful outcomes:
✅ BSc (Hons) Architecture: full five-year RIBA revalidation
✅ MArch Architecture: full five-year RIBA validation

This is the first time both undergraduate and postgraduate courses have both been fully validated by the RIBA and prescribed by the Architects Registration Board (ARB).

The Board commended strong student support, outstanding Springfield facilities, and cohesive community links with practitioners. 🚀
